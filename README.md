## Introduction
My website I have created is a second hand place where customers can browse a range of used second hand BMW cars from the comfort of their own homes. Whether you're jsut looking for a simple car to get to A and B or your a car enthusiast the website has many options available for the customer to choose what is right for them.
## User Experience (UX)
### User Stories (US)
John Barkley is looking for his first car, ideally a used BMW that offers a good balance of reliability and economy. As its his first time buying a car, John has no experience so requires guidance throughout the entire process.
John's story

- **your persona1 story and tasks**
1. "As a first time car buyer I want an easy to navigate website that helps me pre filter owned BMW options so I can narrow down my choices. I need clear and simple explanations of car features, financing options and vehicle histories to ensure im making the right decision. I also want a smooth process for requesting more information or scheduling a test drive, as well as access to customer support for any questions I may have"
